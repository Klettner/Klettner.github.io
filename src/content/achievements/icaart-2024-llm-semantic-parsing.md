---

Investigated the effectiveness of Large Language Models (including GPT-3.5 and LLaMA) in converting natural language dialogue into structured SPARQL queries for knowledge graphs. Demonstrated that few-shot prompting and fine-tuning significantly enhance model accuracy in complex, multi-turn conversational contexts.
